## Status
**Status:** 📋 PLANNED

## 1. Planned Next-Generation Capabilities

### 1. Autonomous Agent-to-Agent Negotiation
* Enables multi-organization AI employees to negotiate delivery schedules, purchase order variances, and contract terms securely over encrypted protocols without human email ping-pong.

### 2. Multi-Modal Fine-Tuning & LoRA Adapters
* In-house fine-tuning pipelines allowing enterprises to train custom LoRA adapters on proprietary engineering schematics and localized financial documents.

### 3. Native SAP NetWeaver / RFC Binary Connectors
* Direct binary RFC protocol support for legacy on-premise SAP ECC and S/4HANA systems without requiring intermediate REST middleware.

### 4. Voice Agent Full-Duplex Real-Time Phone Calling
* WebRTC / SIP telephony gateway enabling the AI employee to make and receive real-time phone calls with customer suppliers to clarify PO delivery delays.
